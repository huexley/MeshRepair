Mesh Repair

Fixes broken 3D-printing models (non-manifold edges, holes, flipped normals, duplicate and degenerate triangles) in STL, 3MF, OBJ and PLY files, entirely offline.

This installs Mesh Repair.app in your Applications folder and the meshrepair command in /usr/local/bin.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.
